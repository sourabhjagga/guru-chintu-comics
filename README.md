# Guru & Chintu: The AI Adventures — Companion Repository

Companion resources for the **Guru & Chintu: The AI Adventures** series by **Sourabh Jagga**.
For kids and beginners, ages **8–17**. English only.

---

## Companion Study Guide — Volume 1 (print-friendly PDF)

**Download:** [`study-guide/Guru-Chintu-Volume-1-Companion-Study-Guide.pdf`](study-guide/Guru-Chintu-Volume-1-Companion-Study-Guide.pdf)

The **Companion Study Guide** is the teaching companion to
*Volume 1: Foundations*. It is **text-only and print-friendly** — it contains
**no comic panel images**, so it prints cleanly in black and white on any home or
school printer.

For all **15 chapters** it includes:

- **Concept notes** — the chapter's ideas in easy language
- **Practical guides & code examples** — the exact commands and Python snippets
- **Practice questions with answers**
- **Talk-about-it discussion questions with answers** (for parents & teachers)
- A short **"How to Use This Guide"** section for running a session

### Who it is for

- **Parents** guiding a child through the book at home
- **Teachers** running a club, a unit, or a single lesson
- **Curious readers (8–17)** who want to check their own understanding

## About the series

*Guru & Chintu: The AI Adventures* follows robot **Guru**, his young friend
**Chintu**, and their little robot companion **Sparky** as they learn how modern
AI actually works — one gentle, funny, hands-on chapter at a time.

- **Volume 1: Foundations** — dev setup, Linux & Git, Python I & II, Docker &
  cloud, linear algebra, calculus & optimization, probability, learning theory,
  regression, classification, clustering, ensembles, evaluation, and time series.
- **Volume 2: Deep Learning & Neural Networks** — coming next.

The comic story and all artwork live in the published book. This repository holds
the companion learning material.
